Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Do I need planning permission for a loft conversion? ▾

In most cases, no. Dormer and Velux roof light conversions usually fall under permitted development rights. Planning permission is more likely if the property is listed, sits in a conservation area, or the dormer is large or overlooks a neighbour. We check this at the free survey and handle any application ourselves.

How long does a loft conversion take? ▾

It depends on the type. A Velux roof light conversion takes around 5 to 8 weeks, a dormer 6 to 8 weeks, a hip to gable 10 to 12 weeks, and an L shaped dormer 12 to 14 weeks. We give you a programme with your quote, not a vague estimate.

Do I have to move out while the work is done? ▾

No. Loft conversions are built using access from the scaffold, so the work stays outside your living space until the very last stage. Bathroom installations are sequenced so you are never left without facilities overnight.

Will a loft conversion add value to my home? ▾

A professionally built and properly signed off conversion can add up to 20% to a property's value, depending on location and layout. It is one of the few improvements that adds a genuine room rather than just a finish.

Can I add an en suite to the loft? ▾

Yes, and most people do. We plan the plumbing runs and the layout at design stage so the soil connection and the head height both work before anything is built.

What is the difference between external wall insulation and just rendering? ▾

Rendering is a finish. External wall insulation is a full system, 90mm EPS insulation board fixed to the wall, basecoat and mesh, a second basecoat, primer, then a 1.5mm silicone render topcoat, with every junction sealed. Render on its own changes how the house looks; the insulation system changes how it performs.

How much heat does external wall insulation actually save? ▾

It reduces heat loss through the walls by roughly 30 to 40%. On a solid brick wall the U value typically improves from around 2.0 W/m²K to 0.28 to 0.30 W/m²K, about an 85% improvement. Properties built before about 1930 usually have solid walls and benefit most.

I have heard external wall insulation can damage a house. Is that true? ▾

Problems come from poor installation or the wrong system for the wall, not from the principle. EPS board is breathable, and finished with silicone render it lets moisture escape while keeping heat in. Fitted correctly it reduces damp and cold spots rather than causing them.

What warranties do you provide? ▾

Ten years on our workmanship across all three trades, plus a fifteen year manufacturer warranty on external wall insulation systems, which is only available because our installers are approved by the system suppliers.

Do you work under ECO4 or government grant schemes? ▾

No. We do not install under ECO4 or government funding schemes, so there is no eligibility assessment and no waiting list. Insulation work is zero rated for VAT, which is applied to your quote automatically.

Can I spread the cost? ▾

Yes. Finance is available through a regulated credit broker at 0% APR over 12 or 24 months, or 12.9% APR over 36, 48, 60 or 120 months, always subject to the lender's assessment. A deposit may be payable and is agreed before you apply. the credit broker is a credit broker, not a lender.

Can you fit a wet room instead of a standard bathroom? ▾

Yes. We design and fit full wet rooms, including the tanking, the floor falls and the drainage. We will walk you through how the waterproofing works before you commit, because that is the part that matters most.
