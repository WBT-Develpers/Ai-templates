Every engineer is directly employed and NAPIT approved, working to BS 7671 wiring regulations. We are also MCS certified for solar and battery, TrustMark registered and RECC members. We will show you the card on the doorstep without being asked.

An Electrical Installation Certificate for new work, a Minor Works Certificate for smaller jobs, or an EICR if we are inspecting an existing installation. Certificates are issued and emailed the same day, not posted weeks later.

An Electrical Installation Condition Report is a formal safety inspection of your fixed wiring. Landlords must hold a valid one every five years by law, and it is strongly recommended for homeowners every ten years, or before you buy a property.

It depends on the size of the property and how much of the existing wiring can stay. We survey first and give you a fixed itemised price with no hidden costs, so the figure you accept is the figure you pay.
