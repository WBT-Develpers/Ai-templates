What we do

Renewable heating, designed around your home

No two properties are the same. We survey first, then recommend the system that suits your building, not the one that suits our stock.

01

Air source heat pumps

Our core work, correctly sized, low flow temperature systems designed around a full heat loss survey rather than a rule of thumb.

  • Retrofit and new build
  • Emitter and cylinder sizing included
  • MCS certified for grant eligibility
02

Ground source heat pumps

Where you have the land, a ground array delivers higher year round efficiency than air source and runs almost silently.

  • Horizontal loops and boreholes
  • Higher SCOP than air source
  • Design and groundworks managed
03

Cylinders & underfloor heating

A heat pump is only as good as what it feeds. We size unvented cylinders and underfloor circuits to run at low temperatures.

  • Unvented hot water cylinders
  • Underfloor heating design and install
  • Larger emitters where needed
04

Boilers & central heating

Not every home is ready for a heat pump yet. Where a boiler is the right answer we will tell you so, and fit it properly.

  • Gas, LPG and oil boilers
  • System upgrades and power flushing
  • Gas Safe registered engineers
05

Solar thermal & MVHR

Complementary technology that lowers the load on your heat pump, hot water from the sun, and ventilation that recovers its own heat.

  • Solar thermal hot water
  • MVHR for airtight homes
  • New build packages
06

Servicing & care plans

Annual servicing keeps efficiency up and manufacturer warranties valid, on our installations and on systems fitted by others.

  • Annual heat pump servicing
  • Fault finding and repairs
  • Care plans with priority response

What we fit

The heat pump we fit, named on your quote

We do not hide behind “premium components”. Every quotation names the exact unit, cylinder and controls you are getting, with the warranty attached to each.

Heat pump units

Daikin Altherma, Mitsubishi Ecodan, Vaillant aroTHERM, Samsung EHS and Panasonic Aquarea, typically 5kW to 16kW, selected on your heat loss figure and the flow temperature your emitters can run at.

Cylinders & buffers

Unvented cylinders sized to your household’s real hot water draw, with heat pump coils rated for low temperature operation, plus volumisers or buffers only where the system genuinely needs one.

Controls & monitoring

Weather compensation set up properly on the day, smart tariff integration for Octopus Cosy and similar, and remote monitoring so we can see a fault developing before you feel it.

Against mains gas on a standard tariff the margin is narrower, but a heat pump specific tariff changes that considerably.

Typically between £5,000 and £15,000 installed, depending on the size of the property, whether emitters need upgrading and whether a new cylinder is required. The £7,500 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant comes off that figure, and domestic installations are zero rated for VAT.
