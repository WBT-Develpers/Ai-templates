What we fit

The heat pump we fit, named on your quote

We do not hide behind "premium components". Every quotation names the exact unit, cylinder and controls you are getting, with the warranty attached to each.

Heat pump units

Daikin Altherma, Mitsubishi Ecodan, Vaillant aroTHERM, Samsung EHS and Panasonic Aquarea, typically 5kW to 16kW, selected on your heat loss figure and the flow temperature your emitters can run at.

Cylinders & buffers

Unvented cylinders sized to your household's real hot water draw, with heat pump coils rated for low temperature operation, plus volumisers or buffers only where the system genuinely needs one.

Controls & monitoring

Weather compensation set up properly on the day, smart tariff integration for Octopus Cosy and similar, and remote monitoring so we can see a fault developing before you feel it.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The questions our surveyors are asked most often. If yours is not here, ring us, we would rather talk it through than sell you something that will not suit your home.

Yes. A correctly specified air source heat pump continues to operate at outdoor temperatures as low as minus 20°C. What matters is the design: a system sized from a full heat loss survey and running at a low flow temperature will keep your home comfortable through a UK cold snap. A system guessed at from the boiler size will not.

It depends on what you are replacing and which tariff you are on. A heat pump produces roughly three to four units of heat for every unit of electricity it consumes, so against oil, LPG or direct electric the saving is usually substantial, up to around 50%. Against mains gas on a standard tariff the margin is narrower, but a heat pump specific tariff changes that considerably.

Typically between £5,000 and £15,000 installed, depending on the size of the property, whether emitters need upgrading and whether a new cylinder is required. Domestic installations are zero rated for VAT.

The outdoor unit needs roughly a metre of clear space on at least two sides for airflow, typically sited on an external wall bracket or a ground level pad. Most gardens, side passages and flat roofs have more than enough room, and we confirm the exact spot with you at survey stage.

Frequently, yes, we have installed successfully in properties over 150 years old. Older buildings usually need larger emitters to run at a lower flow temperature, and sometimes targeted insulation work first. The heat loss survey tells us honestly whether it will work, and we will say so if it will not.

Because it is the difference between a heat pump that works and one that disappoints. A room by room calculation determines the unit size, the radiator sizes and the cylinder. Most heat pumps that underperform were oversized or fitted to emitters that force a high flow temperature, both are design failures, not technology failures.

Most domestic installations take two to four days on site depending on whether emitters and the cylinder are being changed at the same time. We agree the schedule with you in advance and commission the system before we leave.

Yes, annually. Servicing keeps efficiency where it should be and is usually a condition of keeping the manufacturer warranty valid. We service our own installations and systems fitted by other companies.
