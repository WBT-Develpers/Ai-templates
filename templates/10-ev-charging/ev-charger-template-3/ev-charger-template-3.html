Grants and costs

What it costs, and what help is available

Grant rules change often and plenty of installers are vague about them. Here is the straight version, including the part most sites leave out.

£350

Flats and rented homes

The EV chargepoint grant covers up to 75% of the cost, capped at £350, if you own a flat or live in rented accommodation and have off street parking.

£350

On street parking

A separate chargepoint grant for homeowners with no driveway, used alongside a cross pavement cable channel so you can charge from your own supply.

£350

Per workplace socket

The Workplace Charging Scheme pays £350 per socket, up to 40 sockets across all your sites, for businesses, charities and public authorities.

20%

VAT on home installs

Worth knowing: EV chargers do not qualify for the zero rate that applies to solar and heat pumps, so domestic installs carry standard rate VAT.

If you own a house with a driveway, there is no grant, and that is fine

The Electric Vehicle Homecharge Scheme closed to owner occupiers with off street parking in March 2022. Anyone still implying otherwise is either out of date or hoping you will not check. What has replaced it is targeted at flats, renters and homes without a driveway.

A standard 7.4kW smart charger supplied and installed typically lands between £800 and £1,200. Long cable runs, a consumer unit upgrade, groundworks or a cross pavement channel add to that, which is exactly why we survey before quoting rather than after.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

The questions we are asked most often, answered plainly.

How long does an EV charger installation take?
A standard installation takes four to six hours. More complex jobs, such as a long cable run, a consumer unit upgrade or a cross pavement channel, take longer and we will tell you the expected duration before the day rather than on it.
Should I choose a tethered or untethered charger?
Tethered units have the cable permanently attached, so you plug straight in without fetching anything from the boot. They cost a little more and the cable needs hooking up when not in use. Untethered units are a socket you plug your own cable into, which looks cleaner on the wall and lets you swap cables if you change car. If you charge once or twice a week, untethered is rarely an inconvenience.
What is the difference between AC and DC charging?
AC chargers are what you fit at home and at workplaces, where a car is parked for hours at a time. DC chargers convert the supply before it reaches the car, which allows far higher outputs, but they are much more expensive to buy, install and run. That is why you see DC units at motorway services and almost never on a driveway.
Are there any government grants for a home charger?
Only in specific cases. The £350 EV chargepoint grant applies if you own a flat, live in rented accommodation, or have no off street parking. If you own a house with a driveway, the scheme that used to cover you closed in March 2022. Businesses can still claim £350 per socket under the Workplace Charging Scheme.
Can I have a charger if I have no driveway?
Often yes. We fit cross pavement cable gullies, a channel let into the footway so the cable sits flush and nobody trips over it. These need your local authority's permission, which we will help you apply for, and they qualify for the on street chargepoint grant.
Can the charger work with my solar panels?
Yes, if you choose a unit built for it. A Zappi in Eco+ mode will only draw the surplus your panels are exporting, so the car charges on generation rather than import. Hypervolt offers similar solar matching. If you do not have panels yet, we will still fit a charger that can do it later.
Do you notify my electricity network operator?
Always, and it is included. Every charger must be notified to the DNO, and anything above 7.4kW needs their approval before installation rather than after. We handle the paperwork and keep a copy on file for you.
How quickly will my car actually charge?
A 7.4kW charger adds roughly 25 to 30 miles of range per hour, so most cars go from nearly empty to full overnight. That is around three times faster than a three pin plug, which is not designed for sustained charging and should not be used as a permanent solution.
Will I need my consumer unit upgraded?
Sometimes. The charger needs its own protected circuit, and if your board has no spare capacity or lacks the right protective device we will either add a small secondary unit or upgrade the existing one. We establish this at the survey so it is priced in, never sprung on you mid install.
What warranty and guarantee do I get?
The manufacturer warranty on the unit itself, which is three years on most models and up to five on others, plus our own five year workmanship guarantee covering the installation. Both are confirmed in writing when we hand over.
Do you offer finance?
Yes, through Phoenix Finance, typically at 10.9% APR over three to ten years with the full cost breakdown given upfront. Finance is subject to status and credit checks; we act as an introducer and do not give financial advice.
What areas do you cover?
We cover the West Midlands and the surrounding counties, including Wolverhampton, Dudley, Stourbridge, Halesowen and Bridgnorth. If you are just outside, call anyway and we will tell you honestly whether we can reach you.
