08 / Questions

Frequently asked questions

01How much will it cost to replace my boiler?

A straight swap on a like for like combi typically lands between £1,500 and £3,500 fitted, depending on the model, the warranty you want and whether pipework or the flue needs moving. You get a fixed, itemised quotation before anything starts, that figure is what you pay.

02How quickly can my boiler be replaced?

In many cases within 24 hours of accepting the quote, and almost always within a few days. If you are without heating or hot water we will prioritise you and aim to get an engineer out within 12 to 24 hours.

03How much does a boiler service cost?

From £99 + VAT plus parts. Our engineers check, clean and test the boiler and your heating controls, then flag anything that could become a problem. An annual service is the single most effective way to avoid an unexpected breakdown.

04What warranties do you offer?

Up to 12 years on Worcester Bosch, and up to 10 years on Vaillant and Ideal. We are accredited installers for all three, which is what unlocks the longer cover, an unaccredited fitter cannot register those warranties.

05Which boiler brands do you install?

Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Ideal, Baxi, Viessmann, Glow worm, Alpha and Navien. We recommend based on your property, your hot water demand and your budget, not on what we happen to have in the van.

06Do I need a combi, system or regular boiler?

If you have one bathroom and no cylinder, a combi is usually right. Two or more bathrooms, or a household that runs hot taps at the same time, often does better on a system boiler with a cylinder. We confirm it at survey rather than guessing from the old unit.

07How much is a powerflush and do I need one?

£100 per radiator, including valve changes as standard rather than a basic flush. If your radiators are cold at the bottom, your pipework is noisy, or your system is full of sludge, a powerflush is often required before a new boiler warranty is valid.

08Why is my boiler losing pressure?

Usually a leak somewhere on the system, a failed expansion vessel, or a passing pressure relief valve. If you are topping up more than once a month it needs looking at, repressurising repeatedly hides the underlying fault and accelerates corrosion.

09What should I do if my boiler stops working?

Check the pressure gauge, your thermostat and whether other gas appliances still work. If you smell gas, leave the property and ring the National Gas Emergency line on 0800 111 999. Otherwise call us, we carry common parts and fix most faults on the first visit.

10How often should I service my boiler?

Once a year, without exception. It is a condition of virtually every manufacturer warranty, it is a legal requirement for landlords, and it is what keeps the boiler running at the efficiency you paid for.

11Can I pay for a new boiler in instalments?

Yes. Finance is available through an FCA authorised credit broker, with 0% and low rate plans and approval usually in minutes. Finance is subject to status and credit checks; we act as an introducer and do not give financial advice.

12Are your engineers Gas Safe registered?

Every one of them, and we will show you the card without being asked. We are also Which? Trusted Trader assessed and CIPHE members, and your Gas Safety certificate is issued on the day rather than posted weeks later.
