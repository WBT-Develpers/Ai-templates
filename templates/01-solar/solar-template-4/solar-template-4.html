FAQs

How much does a solar panel system cost?
A typical UK residential system runs from around £7,100 for a 3.68kWp array up to £9,695 for 7.36kWp, fully installed. Adding battery storage is roughly £1,200 more depending on capacity. Every quote is fixed price with 0% VAT on domestic installations, and finance is available to spread the cost.
Are solar panels actually worth it in the UK?
Yes, for most homes. A typical household saves around £1,260 a year once solar and battery are combined with the Smart Export Guarantee, which puts payback in the region of six to eight years on a system designed to last 25 to 30. Your surveyor will model your actual figures from your bills rather than a national average.
Do solar panels work on cloudy British days?
They do. Modern panels generate from diffuse daylight, not just direct sun, so an overcast day still produces, just less than a bright one. A home battery smooths this out by storing surplus from sunnier periods for use whenever you need it, day or night.
How long does an installation take?
Most domestic systems are fitted, commissioned and switched on within a single day. Larger arrays, battery retrofits or jobs needing a consumer unit upgrade may run to two. Scaffolding usually goes up the day before and comes down a day or two after, and we agree the whole schedule with you in advance.
Will solar panels work during a power cut?
A standard solar system shuts down in an outage for the safety of engineers working on the network. If keeping power on matters to you, we can specify a battery with whole home backup, such as the Tesla Powerwall 3, which islands your home from the grid and keeps essential circuits running.
Do I need planning permission?
For the vast majority of UK homes, solar falls under permitted development and needs no planning application. Listed buildings and conservation areas are the exception, and we will tell you at survey stage if yours is affected. We handle the DNO notification and MCS registration either way.
What maintenance do solar panels need?
Very little. British rain keeps panels reasonably clean, and there are no moving parts. We recommend a performance check every few years and a clean if output drifts, and our care plans include remote monitoring that flags a fault before you would spot it on your bill.
How do I know which battery size I need?
It comes down to your evening consumption, not your roof. We look at your half hourly usage and size the battery to cover the load between sunset and bedtime, usually 5.8kWh for a smaller home, 8.7kWh for a family, and 14.5kWh where there is an EV or heat pump. Batteries are stackable, so you can add capacity later.
Can you service a system another company installed?
Yes. We repair and maintain solar arrays and battery storage regardless of who fitted them: inverter faults, failed optimisers, monitoring that stopped reporting, or an installer who has since gone out of business. Call us with the make and model and we will tell you honestly whether it is worth repairing.
What is the Smart Export Guarantee?
It is the scheme that pays you for electricity you export back to the grid. Rates vary by supplier, so it is worth shopping around once you are generating. We register your system and provide the MCS certificate you need to claim, so you can start earning from day one.
Do you offer finance?
Yes, payment plans are available on all our packages, typically spread over 24 months, and often the monthly repayment sits close to what you are currently paying your energy supplier. We introduce customers to a regulated credit broker and do not provide financial advice. Finance is subject to status and credit checks.
Will panels damage or leak through my roof?
Not when they are fitted properly. Every roof penetration is flashed and sealed to the same standard as a roofer would use, and we fit bird and pest mesh as standard to stop pigeons nesting underneath. Our workmanship guarantee is insurance backed, so the roof is covered as well as the kit.
