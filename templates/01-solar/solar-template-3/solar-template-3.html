Battery storage

Bank your daytime generation and run the evening on your own sunshine. Retrofit to an existing array or install alongside a new system.

  • 5.8kWh-14.5kWh, stackable
  • Cheap off-peak overnight charging
  • Optional outage backup power

OZEV approved home and workplace charge points, wired to work with your solar so the car charges on surplus generation.
