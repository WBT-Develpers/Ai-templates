Our solutions

Renewable energy for homes, businesses and farms

Whether you're cutting a household bill or powering a working site, every system is sized to real usage and fitted by our own qualified electricians, never a subcontractor.

Most requested

Residential solar PV

Roof mounted solar sized to your household's actual consumption, on tile, slate, flat or metal roofs. In roof and on roof mounting both available.

  • Bird & pest protection fitted as standard
  • Smart Export Guarantee registration
  • 0% VAT on domestic installations

Commercial & agricultural

Larger arrays for warehouses, offices, schools and farm buildings, cutting operating costs and carbon reporting figures at the same time.

  • Roof, ground mount & carports
  • Half hourly usage modelling
  • Full G99 grid application handled

Battery storage

Bank your daytime generation and run the evening on your own sunshine. Retrofit to an existing array or install alongside a new system.

  • 5.8kWh-14.5kWh, stackable
  • Cheap off-peak overnight charging
  • Optional outage backup power

EV charging

OZEV approved home and workplace charge points, wired to work with your solar so the car charges on surplus generation rather than grid import.

  • Domestic & commercial points
  • Solar matched charging
  • 5 year workmanship guarantee

The panels and batteries we fit, named on your quote

Inverters

SolarEdge & Fox ESS hybrid

Panel level optimisation from SolarEdge where shading is a factor, or Fox ESS hybrid inverters where a battery is going in from day one.

  • 3.7kW, 5kW, 6kW hybrid options

Battery storage

Fox ESS ECS 5.8-14.5kWh

Stackable lithium battery towers sized to your evening load, so the electricity you generated at midday still runs the house at nine.

Tesla Powerwall 3

Integrated battery and solar inverter in a single unit, with whole home backup that keeps the lights on through a grid outage.

EV charging

Ohme, Zappi & Hypervolt

OZEV approved 7.4kW charge points that talk to your solar and your tariff, charging the car on surplus generation or cheap overnight units.

They do. Modern panels generate from diffuse daylight, not just direct sun, so an overcast day still produces, just less than a bright one. A home battery smooths this out by storing surplus from sunnier periods for use whenever you need it, day or night.

Most domestic systems are fitted, commissioned and switched on within a single day. Larger arrays, battery retrofits or jobs needing a consumer unit upgrade may run to two. A standard solar system shuts down in an outage for the safety of engineers working on the network. If keeping power on matters to you, we can specify a battery with whole home backup, such as the Tesla Powerwall 3, which islands your home from the grid and keeps essential circuits running.

For the vast majority of UK homes, solar falls under permitted development and needs no planning application. Listed buildings and conservation areas are the exception, and we'll tell you at survey stage if yours is affected. We handle the DNO notification and MCS registration either way.
