What we do

Insulation, rendering and loft work under one roof

Twelve services across the whole building envelope, from the floor void to the ridge.

01

Cavity wall insulation

Fills the gap between the two leaves, where up to 30% of a home's heat escapes.

02

External wall insulation

For solid walls with no cavity. Cuts heat loss by up to 40% and resurfaces the house.

03

Internal wall insulation

Where the outside face has to stay as it is: conservation areas, terraces, one cold room.

04

Loft insulation

The quickest win in most houses: around a quarter of your heat goes out through the roof.

05

Loft boarding

Raised above the insulation so you keep the storage without crushing the performance.

06

Hatches & ladders

Insulated, draught-sealed hatches and a ladder that is safe to actually use.

07

Truss shelving

Storage built into the truss voids in a loft that is too shallow to board out.

08

Room in roof insulation

Rafters, dwarf walls and flat ceilings insulated in an already-converted attic room.

09

Underfloor insulation

Fitted between the joists of a suspended timber floor to stop the draught at ankle height.

10

Cavity extraction

Removing failed or saturated fill so the cavity can dry out and be done again properly.

11

Spray foam removal

Stripped back to clean timbers, the usual reason a lender or surveyor has objected.

12

Render & plastering

Silicone render outside, general plastering inside, finishing what the insulation starts.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to good questions.

It depends entirely on how the wall is built. Most houses put up in the last ninety years have two leaves with a cavity between them, and that cavity can simply be filled. Anything solid, typically pre-1930, has no cavity to fill, so it has to be insulated on the outside or the inside instead. The survey settles which, before anything is priced.

For a typical house, in the region of £8,000 to £12,000. The spread is down to the size of the elevations, the amount of scaffolding, how much detailing there is around windows and doors, and the render finish you choose.

Uninsulated cavity walls can account for around 30% of the heat lost from a home, and an uninsulated roof roughly 25%. External wall insulation typically cuts heat loss through a solid wall by up to 40% and can take up to 35% off a heating bill.

External systems are usually 100-150mm of insulation plus render, so the walls do come out further and window reveals get deeper. Sills, verges and rainwater goods are extended to suit. Most people treat it as a re-skin of the house rather than a repair.

A silicone render system has a working life of roughly 10 to 30 years depending on exposure and how it is looked after. It is self-cleaning to a degree, but a wash down every few years keeps it looking new.

Render needs settled conditions: broadly 5°C to 30°C, no frost forecast and no driving rain while it cures. Winter work is possible with sheeting, but we would rather move a date than apply render into weather that will spoil it.
