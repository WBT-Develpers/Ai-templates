Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to weigh up before booking a survey.

A single wall-mounted unit typically lands between £1,895 and £2,400 fully installed. Multi-room systems running two or three indoor units usually sit between £3,000 and £4,500 depending on pipe runs and access.

Yes. Every system we fit is a reversible heat pump. In heating mode it delivers roughly three to four units of heat for every unit of electricity, which makes it far cheaper to run than plug-in electric heaters and it works well below freezing.

Indoor units start at around 19 dB in night mode, quieter than a whisper and well below a fridge. The outdoor unit sits nearer 45 dB, and we position it to keep noise away from bedroom windows and neighbouring boundaries.

For most English homes a single outdoor unit falls under permitted development, provided it meets the size and siting rules. Listed buildings, conservation areas and flats are the usual exceptions, and we check this for you at survey stage.

A 2.5kW unit cooling a bedroom draws roughly 0.5kW once it reaches temperature, around 13p an hour at current rates. Inverter compressors throttle back rather than cycling on and off, which is where most of the saving comes from.

Once a year. We clean the filters and coils, check refrigerant pressures and test the condensate drain. An annual service keeps the warranty valid and stops efficiency drifting away over time.

Between 22 and 24°C is the sweet spot, cool enough to feel comfortable without the compressor working flat out. Every degree lower adds roughly 5 to 8% to the running cost, so dropping to 18°C on a hot day costs far more than it gains.

Fifteen to twenty years is typical for a system that was sized correctly and serviced annually. Skipping the service is what shortens it. Clogged filters make the compressor work harder, and the compressor is the expensive part.

Yes. We service and repair any make, whoever fitted it. Same-day and next-day appointments are available for breakdowns, and we run a 24/7 out-of-hours line through the summer months.

Our care plan covers the annual service, filter and coil cleaning, refrigerant and pressure checks and priority booking, spread over a manageable monthly payment. It keeps the manufacturer warranty valid and catches small faults before they turn into failures.

We are approved installers for Daikin and Mitsubishi Electric and accredited by Panasonic. We also fit Fujitsu, Toshiba, LG, Samsung, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries and Midea where they suit the room better.

A single wall-mounted unit is usually a one-day job. A two or three room multi-split takes two to three days depending on pipe runs and access. We agree the schedule at survey so you know exactly which days we need to be in the house.
