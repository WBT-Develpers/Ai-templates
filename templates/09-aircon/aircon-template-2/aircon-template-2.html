Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does home air conditioning cost?
A single wall mounted unit is typically £1,895 to £2,400 fitted. A two or three room multi split usually lands between £3,000 and £4,500 depending on pipe runs and access. Every quote is fixed price and itemised, and finance starts around £39 a month.
How much could I save on my running costs?
A 2.5kW unit cooling a bedroom draws roughly 0.5kW once it reaches temperature, around 13p an hour at current rates. Used for heating a single room it is far cheaper than firing the whole central heating system, and up to 50% cheaper than plug in electric heaters.
Do I need planning permission?
For most English homes a single outdoor unit falls under permitted development, provided it meets the size and siting rules. Listed buildings, conservation areas and flats are the usual exceptions, and we check this at survey stage before you commit.
How long does installation take?
A single wall mounted unit is usually a one day job. A two or three room multi split takes two to three days depending on pipe runs, and concealed ducted work longer. We agree the schedule at survey so you know which days we need access.
Can it heat my home in winter as well?
Yes. Every system we fit is a reversible heat pump, so the same unit heats in winter. It delivers roughly three to four units of heat for each unit of electricity and keeps working well below freezing.
How often does it need servicing?
Once a year. We clean filters and coils, check refrigerant pressures and test the condensate drain. An annual service keeps the manufacturer warranty valid and stops efficiency drifting away.
Do you repair systems you did not install?
Yes, any make and whoever fitted it. Same day and next day appointments are available for breakdowns, and we run a 24/7 out of hours line through the summer months.
How noisy is a modern system?
Indoor units start around 19 dB in night mode, quieter than a whisper. The outdoor unit sits nearer 45 dB, about the same as a fridge, and we position it away from bedroom windows and boundaries.
Which brands do you install?
We are approved installers for Daikin and Mitsubishi Electric and accredited by Panasonic. We also fit Fujitsu, Toshiba, LG, Samsung and Mitsubishi Heavy Industries where they suit the room better.
Do you offer a maintenance plan?
Our care plan covers the annual service, filter and coil cleaning, refrigerant checks and priority booking, spread over a manageable monthly payment. It keeps the manufacturer warranty valid and catches faults early.
