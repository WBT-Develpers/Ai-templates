Frequently asked questions

A single wall mounted unit is typically £1,895 to £2,400 fitted. A two or three room multi split usually lands between £3,000 and £4,500 depending on pipe runs and access. Every quote is fixed price and itemised, and finance starts around £39 a month.

A 2.5kW unit cooling a bedroom draws roughly 0.5kW once it reaches temperature, around 13p an hour at current rates. Used for heating a single room it is far cheaper than firing the whole central heating system, and up to 50% cheaper than plug in electric heaters.

For most English homes a single outdoor unit falls under permitted development, provided it meets the size and siting rules. Listed buildings, conservation areas and flats are the usual exceptions, and we check this at survey stage before you commit.

A single wall mounted unit is usually a one day job. A two or three room multi split takes two to three days depending on pipe runs, and concealed ducted work longer. We agree the schedule at survey so you know which days we need access.

Yes. Every system we fit is a reversible heat pump, so the same unit heats in winter. It delivers roughly three to four units of heat for each unit of electricity and keeps working well below freezing.
