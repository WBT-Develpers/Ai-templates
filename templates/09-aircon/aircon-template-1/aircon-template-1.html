What we fit

The units we fit

We are approved installers for Daikin and Mitsubishi Electric and accredited by Panasonic. These are the ranges we fit most often, which one suits you depends on the room, not on what happens to be in the van.

Daikin

Sensira FTXF

The value benchmark, and the unit we fit most in bedrooms and home offices. Quiet, efficient and simple to live with.

  • 2.0 to 5.0kW, A+++ cooling
  • 19 dB minimum sound level
  • Optional Wi Fi controller

Daikin

Stylish FTXA

Flat fronted and only 189mm deep, for rooms where a standard unit would dominate the wall. Silver, white or matt black.

  • 2.0 to 3.5kW, A+++ both modes
  • Built in Wi Fi and presence sensor
  • Flash Streamer air purification

Mitsubishi Electric

MSZ AP Series

Where reliability matters most. Dual barrier coating keeps the internals clean, which is what keeps efficiency where it started.

  • 2.5 to 7.1kW single split
  • Works down to minus 15°C in heating
  • MELCloud app control

Multi room

Multi Split & Ducted

One outdoor condenser running up to five indoor units, each room on its own temperature. Where you would rather see nothing at all, we fit concealed ducted or ceiling cassettes.

  • Up to 5 indoor units per condenser
  • Wall, floor, cassette or ducted
  • Zoned scheduling per room

Frequently asked questions

A single wall mounted unit is typically £1,895 to £2,400 fitted. A two or three room multi split usually lands between £3,000 and £4,500 depending on pipe runs and access. Every quote is fixed price and itemised, and finance starts around £39 a month.

A 2.5kW unit cooling a bedroom draws roughly 0.5kW once it reaches temperature, around 13p an hour at current rates. Used for heating a single room it is far cheaper than firing the whole central heating system, and up to 50% cheaper than plug in electric heaters.

For most English homes a single outdoor unit falls under permitted development, provided it meets the size and siting rules. Listed buildings, conservation areas and flats are the usual exceptions, and we check this at survey stage before you commit.

A single wall mounted unit is usually a one day job. A two or three room multi split takes two to three days depending on pipe runs, and concealed ducted work longer. We agree the schedule at survey so you know which days we need access.
