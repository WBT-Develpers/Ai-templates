What we fit

The units we fit

We are approved installers for Daikin and Mitsubishi Electric and accredited by Panasonic. These are the ranges we fit most often. Which one suits you depends on the room, not on what happens to be in the van.

Daikin

Sensira FTXF

The value benchmark, and the unit we fit most in bedrooms and home offices. Quiet, efficient and simple to live with.

  • 2.0-5.0kW, A+++ cooling
  • 19 dB minimum sound level
  • Optional Wi-Fi controller

Daikin

Stylish FTXA

Flat-fronted and only 189mm deep, for rooms where a standard unit would dominate the wall. Silver, white or matt black.

  • 2.0-3.5kW, A+++ both modes
  • Built-in Wi-Fi and presence sensor

Mitsubishi Electric

MSZ-AP Series

Where reliability matters most. Dual barrier coating keeps the internals clean, which is what keeps efficiency where it started.

  • MELCloud app control

Weak airflow, warm air on a cooling cycle, water pooling beneath the indoor unit or a new rattle from the condenser are the four most common signals. Any of them warrants a call before the compressor is stressed further.

Substantially. An inverter compressor modulates rather than cycling on and off, so it runs at part load almost permanently, the condition in which it is quietest and most efficient, typically 30-40% below a fixed-speed equivalent.

A single wall-mounted unit is typically £1,895 to £2,400 fitted. A two or three room multi-split usually lands between £3,000 and £4,500 depending on pipe runs and access. Every quote is fixed and itemised, and finance starts around £39 a month.

Yes. Every system we fit is a reversible heat pump, so the same unit heats in winter, delivering roughly three to four units of heat per unit of electricity. For a single room it is far cheaper than firing the whole central heating system.

Between 22 and 24°C is the sweet spot. Every degree lower adds roughly 5 to 8% to the running cost, so setting it to 18°C on a hot day costs far more than it gains in comfort.

Fifteen to twenty years for a system that was sized correctly and serviced annually. Skipping the service is what shortens it. Blocked filters make the compressor work harder, and the compressor is the expensive part.

Once a year. We clean filters and coils, check refrigerant pressures and test the condensate drain. An annual service keeps the manufacturer warranty valid and stops efficiency drifting away.

Our care plan covers the annual service, filter and coil cleaning, refrigerant and pressure checks and priority booking, spread over a manageable monthly payment. It keeps your warranty valid and catches small faults early.

For most English homes a single outdoor unit falls under permitted development, provided it meets the size and siting rules. Listed buildings, conservation areas and flats are the usual exceptions, and we check this at survey stage.
